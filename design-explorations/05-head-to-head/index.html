Head to Head

Ranking eight things on a phone is work. Choosing between two is easy. Voters answer a few "this or that?" matchups and Rankzap sorts their ballot for them, about n·log n questions instead of a long drag. Results show the actual matchups, which is what Copeland counts anyway.

Type

Instrument Serif for options and headlines (options become the stars), Instrument Sans for interface text.

Interaction and motion

  • Tap or swipe toward a side. The winner swells, the other slides off, the next pair rises in about 300ms.
  • "Your ranking so far" builds below with a dashed slot showing where the current option is being placed.
  • Drag mode stays one tap away for people who'd rather order the list directly. Same ballot data either way.
