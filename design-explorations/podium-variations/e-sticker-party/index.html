Sticker party

Podium turned up for parties and classrooms. Every option is a die-cut sticker: thick outline, hard shadow, slightly crooked. Pick one up and it wiggles; submit and a "Voted!" sticker slaps onto the button. The most fun, and the loudest. It works best as a theme you choose for a poll, not the whole app.

Yellow Pink Sky Mint Orange Outline

What changes from Podium

Square blocks become rounded stickers with 3px outlines and hard 4px shadows. Places 1–3 get pink, sky and mint; numerals sit in round badges.

Motion

  • Pick-up: scale 1.06, tilt −3°, a gentle wiggle while held. Drop: spring back to its crooked rest angle.
  • Buttons press down into their shadow instead of scaling.
  • Submit slaps an orange "Voted!" sticker on with overshoot; a new leader's name slaps in the same way.
