Floodlight

Podium after dark. Same staircase, same springs, but the ground goes black and the signal yellow becomes the floodlight: it only falls on your first pick, the leader and the main action. Whenever the leader changes, the lights flash white and come back up. It doubles as Podium's dark theme, so the app can keep following the system setting.

What changes from Podium

Yellow goes from the background to a single light source. Everything else sits in three greys, so the hierarchy is "lit or not".

Motion

  • Leader change: the winner block flashes white for 900ms, like stadium lights.
  • Dragging shows a yellow outline plus a deep shadow instead of the hard offset (offsets don't read on black).
  • Live dot blinks in steps, not a soft pulse: a scoreboard, not a notification.
