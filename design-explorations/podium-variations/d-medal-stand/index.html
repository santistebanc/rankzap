Medal stand

Take the name literally. Your top three stand on a real 2-1-3 podium that rebuilds itself as you drag, and the live results are a podium whose step heights follow the scores. Everything below third place sits on the bench. The most game-like Podium, and the easiest to understand on a projector.

What changes from Podium

The staircase becomes an actual podium. Medal colours appear only as small discs, so the yellow and black poster still dominates.

Motion

  • Drag a new option into the top three and its name drops onto the step from above.
  • Live results: step heights spring to their new scores; a lead change swaps names on the stand.
  • On close (not shown): a confetti-free "medal ceremony", where the steps rise one at a time, 3rd, 2nd, then 1st.
