Election Night

Every poll is a live broadcast. The results screen is the main event: a scoreboard that recounts on each vote, a crawl of who just voted, and a method switch that shows how Borda, Dowdall and Copeland can call the race differently. Made to be shown on a big screen while people vote on their phones.

Night Panel Ink Muted Live Gain

Type

Barlow Condensed for scores, names and headlines (scoreboard numerals, tabular); Barlow for sentences.

Interaction and motion

  • The ballot shows what each slot is worth under the poll's method, so voters understand the count while ranking.
  • Scores roll up from below on change; a green "+n" flashes and fades; rows glide to their new place over 600ms.
  • Presence as a crowd strip (voted, ranking now) and a news crawl of events. Both stop for reduced motion.
