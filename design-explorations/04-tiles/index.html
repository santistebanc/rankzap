Tiles

Calm, tactile and one-handed. Options are physical tiles: you pick them up, they lift and the others make room. Only the ones you care about go into your tray, so a ballot with 15 options doesn't mean ranking all 15. Closest to a polished native app.

Ground Tile Sunk Ink Cobalt

Type

Geist only, tight negative tracking on titles, tabular figures for scores. No uppercase anywhere.

Interaction and motion

  • Picked-up tile scales to 1.04 with a real shadow (the only shadow in the system); the rest sink to 0.985. Spring curves, interruptible.
  • Tray and pile: tap + to rank, − to drop. Partial ballots use the existing "unranked = 0" rule.
  • Results open with "who people put first", then the full count. Light haptic tick on each slot change.
