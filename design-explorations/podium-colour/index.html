Your colour

The original Podium for light mode, Floodlight for dark, and the host picks the poll's colour when making it. In light mode the colour is the whole ground, the original's yellow made choosable. In dark mode it becomes the floodlight on your first pick, the leader and the main button. Voters see the poll's colour from the first tap of the link. Pick a colour in either "New poll" phone: every phone below repaints, in both themes.

The colour set

Eight curated signals, each with its own text colour (black, or white on cobalt) and a lighter variant for use as text on black. Every pairing stays readable in both themes, which a free colour picker can't promise. Yellow stays the default, so an unconfigured poll looks like the brand.

Interaction and motion

  • Tap a swatch: the new colour floods out from your finger in a circle over about 550ms, then the poll is that colour everywhere.
  • Light and dark follow the device setting; the poll colour carries across both.
  • Everything else is the original Podium: staircase ballot, springs, numeral punch, and Floodlight's white flash on a new leader.

Data

One new setting next to the existing ones, e.g. settings.colour: "yellow" | "cobalt" | …. The voter page reads it before first paint so there's no flash of yellow.
