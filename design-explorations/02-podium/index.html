Podium

Loud, flat and physical. The rank is the layout: first place is the biggest block on the screen, second is smaller, and so on down a staircase. Drag something up and it grows as it climbs. Built for group chats, classrooms and parties, where a poll should feel like a game.

Signal Black White Bench

Type

Archivo only, swinging its width axis: 62% condensed caps for numerals and headlines, normal width for sentences. Numerals are the hero graphic.

Interaction and motion

  • Rows resize by place (108 / 84 / 70 / 58px) so the ranking reads at a glance from across a room.
  • Springy overshoot on every swap and drop; rank numerals punch when they change. Hard offset shadow instead of a soft one while dragging.
  • Results lead with a full-bleed winner; score bands overshoot into place on each new vote.
