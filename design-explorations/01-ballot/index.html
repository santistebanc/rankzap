Ballot Paper

Rankzap as a printed ballot. Every poll is a sheet with a serial number (its room code), you number the boxes in pen, and casting it gets stamped. The trust of a real ballot, with live counting underneath.

Stock Sheet Ink Band Pen Stamp

Type

Newsreader for titles and figures, Hanken Grotesk for interface text, Caveat only for what the voter "writes": rank numbers, their name, signatures.

Interaction and motion

  • Tap an empty box to number it next; drag to change your mind. Partial ballots are allowed (unranked scores 0).
  • Numbers write themselves in left to right; casting thumps a red stamp with time and ballot number.
  • Results look like a tally sheet; the winner gets circled in red pen when the poll closes.
